# Government Agricultural Schemes — India & Karnataka

## Central Government Schemes

### 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Benefit**: Rs 6,000 per year (Rs 2,000 every 4 months) directly to bank account
- **Eligibility**: All landholding farmer families. Land must be in farmer's name. Aadhaar mandatory.
- **Exclusion**: Income tax payers, government employees, professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers)
- **Documents**: Aadhaar, bank passbook, land records (RTC/pahani)
- **Apply**: pmkisan.gov.in or nearest CSC centre
- **Helpline**: 155261

### 2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Benefit**: Crop insurance against drought, flood, pest, natural calamity. Sum insured = district average yield × MSP
- **Premium**: Kharif crops 2%, Rabi crops 1.5%, horticulture 5% (farmer's share — rest paid by govt)
- **Eligibility**: All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas. Both loanee and non-loanee farmers.
- **Documents**: Land records, bank account, sowing certificate, Aadhaar
- **Apply**: pmfby.gov.in or nearest bank branch before crop cutoff date
- **Important**: Apply before sowing cutoff — usually 2 weeks after sowing season starts

### 3. Soil Health Card Scheme
- **Benefit**: Free soil testing + personalised fertilizer recommendation card every 2 years
- **Eligibility**: All farmers
- **Documents**: Land details, Aadhaar
- **Apply**: Nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agriculture department office
- **Use**: Follow card recommendations to reduce fertilizer cost by 20–30%

### 4. PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
- **Benefit**: Rs 3,000 per month pension after age 60
- **Eligibility**: Small and marginal farmers (land ≤ 2 hectares), age 18–40, not covered by other pension schemes
- **Contribution**: Rs 55–200/month (age-based), government contributes equal amount
- **Documents**: Aadhaar, bank account, land records
- **Apply**: Nearest CSC centre or maandhan.in

### 5. Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- **Benefit**: Credit up to Rs 3 lakh at 4% interest rate (after 2% interest subvention)
- **Eligibility**: All farmers, sharecroppers, tenant farmers, SHG members
- **Documents**: Land records, identity proof, passport photo
- **Apply**: Any nationalised bank, cooperative bank, or RRB

### 6. eNAM (National Agriculture Market)
- **Benefit**: Sell crops online across India, get better price discovery
- **Eligibility**: All farmers with produce to sell
- **Apply**: enam.gov.in, register with nearest APMC

## Karnataka State Schemes

### 7. Raitha Siri Scheme
- **Benefit**: Rs 2,000 per year additional to PM-Kisan (Karnataka supplement)
- **Eligibility**: Karnataka farmers registered under PM-Kisan
- **Documents**: PM-Kisan registration, Aadhaar, Karnataka domicile
- **Apply**: Nearest Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK)

### 8. Bhoochetana Scheme
- **Benefit**: Integrated soil fertility management + free inputs (micronutrients, biofertilizers) + training
- **Eligibility**: Farmers in targeted districts of Karnataka
- **Apply**: District Agriculture Officer

### 9. Krishi Bhagya Scheme
- **Benefit**: Farm pond construction, pump sets, drip irrigation at 90% subsidy for SC/ST farmers, 80% for others
- **Eligibility**: Farmers with land, priority to rain-fed areas
- **Documents**: Land records, caste certificate, bank account
- **Apply**: Nearest Raitha Samparka Kendra

### 10. Karnataka Raitha Suraksha Crop Loan Waiver
- **Benefit**: Crop loan waiver up to Rs 2 lakh for eligible farmers
- **Eligibility**: Small and marginal farmers with overdue crop loans
- **Apply**: Through respective bank with land and loan documents

### 11. CMAF — Chief Minister's Agriculture Fund
- **Benefit**: Interest-free loans for farm equipment, drip irrigation, greenhouse
- **Eligibility**: Karnataka farmers with valid land records
- **Apply**: District Agriculture Department

## Scheme Eligibility Quick Guide

| Farmer Type | Land | Income | Recommended Schemes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small/Marginal | < 2 hectares | < 1.5 lakh | PM-Kisan, PMFBY, KCC, PM-KMY, Raitha Siri, Krishi Bhagya |
| Medium | 2–5 hectares | 1.5–3 lakh | PM-Kisan, PMFBY, KCC, Soil Health Card, eNAM |
| Large | > 5 hectares | > 3 lakh | PMFBY, KCC, Soil Health Card, eNAM |
| SC/ST Farmer | Any | Any | All above + 90% subsidy on Krishi Bhagya + special NABARD schemes |
| Women Farmer | Any | Any | All above + priority in SHG-linked KCC |
