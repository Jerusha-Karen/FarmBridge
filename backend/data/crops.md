# Karnataka Crop Knowledge Base

## Crop Calendar — Karnataka

### Kharif Season (June–November)
- **Rice (Paddy)**: Sow June–July, harvest October–November. Districts: Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, Hassan, Shivamogga
- **Jowar**: Sow June–July, harvest September–October. Districts: Vijayapura, Bagalkot, Kalaburagi
- **Maize**: Sow June–July, harvest September. Districts: Davangere, Haveri, Chitradurga
- **Cotton**: Sow June–July, harvest November–January. Districts: Dharwad, Gadag, Haveri
- **Groundnut**: Sow June–July, harvest October. Districts: Kalaburagi, Raichur, Vijayapura
- **Soybean**: Sow June–July, harvest October. Districts: Belagavi, Dharwad

### Rabi Season (November–April)
- **Wheat**: Sow November–December, harvest March–April. Districts: Bidar, Kalaburagi
- **Sunflower**: Sow November, harvest February–March. Districts: Raichur, Koppal, Ballari
- **Chickpea (Bengal Gram)**: Sow October–November, harvest February. Districts: Kalaburagi, Bidar
- **Safflower**: Sow October–November, harvest February. Districts: Vijayapura, Bagalkot

### Summer Season (March–June)
- **Watermelon**: Sow February–March, harvest May–June
- **Vegetables**: Tomato, brinjal, chilli year-round in Hassan, Kolar, Chikkaballapur

## Common Pests and Management

### Rice Pests
- **Brown Plant Hopper (BPH)**: Yellow patches, wilting. Use neem-based pesticide, avoid excess nitrogen. Spray Imidacloprid 17.8 SL at 0.3ml/litre.
- **Stem Borer**: Dead heart in vegetative stage, white ear in reproductive stage. Use Cartap Hydrochloride 4G at 18kg/hectare.
- **Leaf Folder**: Folded leaves with white streaks. Spray Chlorpyriphos 20 EC at 2ml/litre.
- **Blast Disease (fungal)**: Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves. Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP at 0.6g/litre. Avoid excess nitrogen.

### Tomato Pests
- **Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa)**: Holes in fruit. Spray Spinosad 45 SC at 0.3ml/litre. Use pheromone traps.
- **Leaf Curl Virus**: Curled, yellowing leaves, spread by whiteflies. Remove infected plants. Use yellow sticky traps. Spray Thiamethoxam 25 WG.
- **Early Blight**: Brown spots with rings on older leaves. Spray Mancozeb 75 WP at 2.5g/litre.
- **Late Blight**: Water-soaked lesions, white mold. Spray Metalaxyl + Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre.

### Cotton Pests
- **Bollworm**: Holes in bolls. Use Bt cotton, pheromone traps, spray Indoxacarb 14.5 SC.
- **Whitefly**: Yellowing leaves, sticky residue. Spray Pyriproxyfen 5 EC at 1ml/litre. Avoid broad-spectrum pesticides.
- **Aphids**: Curled leaves, stunted growth. Spray neem oil 5ml/litre or Dimethoate 30 EC at 1.5ml/litre.

### Groundnut Pests
- **Leaf Miner**: Serpentine mines on leaves. Spray Triazophos 40 EC at 2ml/litre.
- **Tikka Disease (leaf spot)**: Brown spots with yellow halos. Spray Chlorothalonil 75 WP at 2g/litre.

## Soil Health

### Soil Types in Karnataka
- **Red Laterite Soil**: Coastal Karnataka (DK, Udupi). Low fertility, acidic. Add lime, organic matter.
- **Black Cotton Soil (Regur)**: Northern Karnataka (Vijayapura, Bagalkot). High water retention. Good for cotton, jowar.
- **Red Sandy Loam**: Central Karnataka (Davangere, Chitradurga). Moderate fertility. Good for groundnut, sunflower.
- **Alluvial Soil**: River valleys. High fertility. Good for vegetables, banana.

### Fertilizer Recommendations (per acre)
- **Rice**: 50kg Urea + 250kg SSP + 50kg MOP + 25kg ZnSO4 (for zinc-deficient soils)
- **Wheat**: 65kg Urea + 375kg SSP + 33kg MOP
- **Cotton**: 65kg Urea + 250kg SSP + 33kg MOP, split in 3 doses
- **Tomato**: 65kg Urea + 500kg SSP + 83kg MOP + FYM 4 tonnes/acre
- **Groundnut**: 16kg Urea + 500kg SSP + 33kg MOP (groundnut fixes its own nitrogen)

### Organic Farming Tips
- Apply Farm Yard Manure (FYM) 5–10 tonnes/acre before sowing
- Use Vermicompost 1–2 tonnes/acre for vegetable crops
- Green manure: Sow Dhaincha or Sunhemp, incorporate before flowering
- Biofertilizers: Rhizobium for legumes, Azospirillum for cereals, PSB for all crops

## Water Management

### Irrigation Schedules
- **Rice**: Keep 5cm standing water during tillering. Drain 10 days before harvest.
- **Cotton**: Critical stages — flowering and boll development. Irrigate every 10–12 days.
- **Drip Irrigation**: Recommended for tomato, chilli, banana. Saves 40–50% water vs flood irrigation.
- **Sprinkler**: Recommended for groundnut, vegetables, pulses.

### Rainwater Harvesting
- Farm ponds: PMKSY scheme covers 50% cost for farm pond construction
- Bunds and contour trenches: Prevent runoff on sloped land
