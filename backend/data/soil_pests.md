# Soil Health and Pest Management — Karnataka

## Disease Identification Guide

### Yellowing Leaves (Chlorosis)
- **Nitrogen deficiency**: Uniform yellowing from older leaves upward. Apply urea top dressing.
- **Iron deficiency**: Yellowing between veins, veins stay green (interveinal chlorosis). Apply FeSO4 spray 0.5%.
- **Zinc deficiency**: Small leaves, shortened internodes, brown spots. Apply ZnSO4 10kg/acre.
- **Waterlogging**: Uniform yellowing + root rot. Improve drainage immediately.
- **Viral infection**: Mosaic pattern yellowing + leaf curl. No cure — remove plant, control vector insects.

### Wilting Plants
- **Water stress**: Wilt during afternoon, recover at night. Irrigate immediately.
- **Fusarium wilt (fungal)**: Permanent wilt, vascular discoloration inside stem. Use resistant varieties, soil drench with Carbendazim.
- **Root rot**: Blackened roots, foul smell. Improve drainage, apply Trichoderma to soil.
- **Stem borer**: Dead heart symptom in rice/maize. Pull central shoot — comes out easily. Apply Carbofuran 3G.

### Spots on Leaves
- **Early blight (fungal)**: Concentric rings, brown spots on older leaves. Spray Mancozeb 2.5g/litre.
- **Late blight (fungal)**: Water-soaked irregular spots, white mold on underside. Spray Metalaxyl+Mancozeb.
- **Bacterial leaf spot**: Angular water-soaked spots, yellow halo, no mold. Spray Copper oxychloride 3g/litre.
- **Rust**: Orange-brown powdery pustules on leaves. Spray Propiconazole 1ml/litre.

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

### Prevention First
1. Use certified disease-free seeds
2. Treat seeds with Thiram 75 WP at 3g/kg before sowing
3. Deep plough after harvest to expose and kill soil pests
4. Maintain field hygiene — remove crop residues
5. Use resistant varieties wherever available

### Biological Control
- **Trichoderma viride**: Soil-borne diseases. Apply 2kg/acre with FYM.
- **Pseudomonas fluorescens**: Bacterial diseases. Seed treatment + soil application.
- **Trichogramma cards**: Egg parasitoid for bollworm, fruit borer. Release 1 card/acre/week.
- **Neem cake**: 100kg/acre in soil controls nematodes and soil insects.

### Chemical Control — Safety Rules
- Always wear gloves, mask, and full-sleeve clothes while spraying
- Spray in early morning or evening — avoid hot afternoon hours
- Do not spray within 2 weeks of harvest (pre-harvest interval)
- Store pesticides in original containers, locked away from children
- Never eat, drink, or smoke while handling pesticides
- Wash hands and face thoroughly after spraying

## Soil Testing and Improvement

### When to Test Soil
- Before sowing every 2 years
- If crop growth is poor despite good care
- After a flood or unusually heavy rain season
- Before shifting to a new crop

### How to Collect Soil Sample
1. Collect from 8–10 spots in your field in a zigzag pattern
2. Remove surface litter and take soil from 0–20cm depth
3. Mix all samples in a clean bucket
4. Take 500g of mixed soil in a clean bag
5. Label with your name, survey number, crop, and date
6. Submit to nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra or Soil Testing Lab — free of charge

### Improving Soil Health
- **Acidic soil (pH < 6)**: Apply agricultural lime 200–400kg/acre
- **Alkaline soil (pH > 8)**: Apply gypsum 200kg/acre + organic matter
- **Compacted soil**: Deep plough once every 3 years, add organic matter
- **Sandy soil (low water retention)**: Add FYM 5 tonnes/acre, consider mulching
- **Waterlogged black soil**: Make raised beds, improve drainage channels
